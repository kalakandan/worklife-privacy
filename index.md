Work & Life ("the app") is a planner for shift and irregular-schedule workers. This policy explains,
in plain language, exactly what the app does with your information. It describes the app **as it
exists today** — if that ever changes, this policy changes first, and you will be asked to review it
again before the app continues.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

Work & Life is developed and published by **Kalakandan**, an individual developer based in the
**United Arab Emirates**. For the limited data described in section 4, that person is the
**data controller**.

Contact: **imkalakandan@gmail.com**

## 2. Short version

- Everything you enter stays **on your device**. There are no accounts, no logins, and no server
  operated by us. We cannot see your data, because we never receive it.
- The **only** reason the app uses the internet is to show one banner advertisement, served by
  **Google AdMob**.
- To show that ad, Google collects limited device data — described in full below.
- The app asks you before any of that happens, and you can change your answer at any time (section 8).

## 3. The information you enter stays on your device

Everything you enter — shift pattern, schedule changes, leave, habits, tasks, spending, pay settings,
medication and family reminders, and sleep preferences — is stored **only in the app's private
storage on your own phone**. The database holding it is encrypted at rest. A small amount of app
state sits alongside it in the app's private area without that extra layer — the answers you give
during setup before you finish it, and the details of an alarm you have snoozed — where Android
prevents other apps from reading it. None of it is ever uploaded to a server of ours, because we do
not operate one. The app's backup rules explicitly exclude all of this from cloud backup and
device-to-device transfer.

We never receive, see, store, or have any technical means of accessing it.

## 4. Information collected automatically (advertising only)

The app displays one banner ad using **Google AdMob**. To select and measure that ad, the Google
Mobile Ads SDK allows Google to collect and process:

- your device's **advertising ID** (a resettable identifier used for advertising);
- your **IP address**, from which an **approximate (city-level) location** can be inferred;
- basic **device information** (model, operating system version, language, screen size);
- **ad-interaction data** (that an ad was shown, viewed, or tapped);
- **diagnostics about the ad component itself** (performance and error signals from Google's ad
  software — for example that an ad failed to load).

This is collected by **Google acting as a third party**, under Google's own policies. It is **never
combined** with the schedule, health, or financial information you enter — that data never leaves
your phone.

**Advertising partners.** Google AdMob is the **only** third party that receives anything from this
app. There is no mediation network, no ad exchange, and no other third-party SDK.

**To be precise about "analytics":** we add **no analytics or crash-reporting software of our own** —
there is no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Crashlytics, and nothing that reports your use of the
app to us. We receive no reports at all. Google's own ad software does, however, perform its own
measurement and analytics on the advertising data listed above, for the purposes in section 5.

Google's policies:
- How Google uses information from sites/apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Why we collect it, and our legal basis

The data in section 4 is collected **only for the advertising that keeps the app free**, and only by
Google. Specifically, Google uses it to:

- **select and display** the banner advertisement;
- **measure and analyse** advertising performance (whether an ad was shown, seen, or tapped);
- **detect fraud and invalid traffic** (for example, fake or automated ad clicks).

These are the same purposes declared in this app's **Data Safety** disclosure on Google Play; the two
are meant to be read together and must always agree. The data is used for **no other purpose**, it is
**not used by us**, and it is never used to build a profile of you for anything other than the ad above.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis is your **consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)), obtained before any ad
request is made. Where consent is not the applicable basis in your region, we rely on the choices you
make in the prompts described in section 8.

## 6. International transfers

Google processes advertising data on servers outside your country, including in the **United
States**. Those transfers are made under Google's own safeguards (EU Standard Contractual Clauses
and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), as described in Google's privacy policy linked above.

## 7. How long it is kept

**We keep nothing** — we operate no server and receive no data, so we have no retention period to
state. Data you enter lives on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app. Advertising data
described in section 4 is retained by **Google** according to Google's own retention policies.

## 8. Your choices and how to change them

- **Before any ad loads**, the app shows you a disclosure and asks you to agree. Nothing is requested
  from Google — not even the check for whether a consent form is needed — until you do.
- **Withdrawing.** You can reopen your ad privacy choices at any time from **Settings → About → Ad
  privacy choices**. Withdrawing is as easy as agreeing. Withdrawing does not remove ads; it means the
  ads you see are **not personalised**.
- **Resetting your ad ID.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's
  system settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
- **If this policy materially changes**, the app will ask you to review and agree again. Your previous
  agreement is **not** carried over to a new version — an agreement covers the disclosure that was on
  screen at the time, and nothing else.

## 9. US state privacy rights (California and others)

**We do not receive money in exchange for your personal information.** However, we would rather be
accurate than reassuring: because the app shares an advertising ID with Google for personalised
advertising, this activity **may qualify as a "sale" or as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral
advertising** under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) and similar laws in
other US states.

You can opt out. Use **Settings → About → Ad privacy choices**, or reset or delete your advertising
ID in your phone's system settings. We do not discriminate against you for exercising this right.

We do not knowingly process sensitive personal information for advertising purposes.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to **access**, **correct**, **delete**, **object
to**, or **restrict** processing of your personal data, to **data portability**, and to **withdraw
consent** at any time.

Because we operate no server and hold no data about you, the practical position is:

- **Data you entered:** already entirely under your control. Export it to a file from within the app,
  or uninstall the app to remove it from your device.
- **Advertising data:** held by **Google**, not by us. Exercise those rights with Google directly at
  https://myadcenter.google.com and https://myaccount.google.com/privacy.
- **Anything else:** email **imkalakandan@gmail.com** and we will respond.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you also have the right to **lodge a complaint with your
national data protection supervisory authority**.

## 11. Permissions the app uses, and why

- **Internet:** used **only** to load the Google AdMob banner.
- **Alarms & reminders** (exact alarm, notifications, full-screen alarm, run at startup, ignore
  battery optimisations, vibrate, foreground service): to fire your wake and "leave for your shift"
  alarms and reminders at the right time, show the ringing alarm over the lock screen, and restore
  them after a restart. Used entirely on-device.
- **Voice input (optional):** when you tap a microphone button, your speech is handed to your phone's
  **own built-in speech recognition** so you can dictate instead of type. The system feature handles
  the microphone; **the app itself holds no microphone permission and never records, stores, or
  transmits any audio.**
- **Calendar:** the app holds the Android calendar permissions but **no longer offers any feature
  that uses them** — the "Device calendar sync" option has been removed. The permissions remain only
  so the app can tidy up (delete) a calendar an earlier version created on your phone. Nothing was
  ever, and nothing is, sent to us.

The app requests **no location, camera, contacts, or storage permissions**.

## 12. Sharing

When you use a "Share" feature (your roster, your free evenings, a backup file, or an "I'm home"
message), Android's own share sheet opens and **you choose** where it goes. The app itself never
transmits your entered information automatically.

## 13. Children

The app is a general-purpose planner and is **not directed at children**. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. Aside from the standard advertising data described in section 4,
it collects no personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information,
contact us and we will act.

## 14. Security

Data you enter is held in the app's private storage and excluded from cloud backup and device
transfer. The database is encrypted at rest, with the key held in the device's own secure key store.
A small amount of app state — unfinished setup answers, and snoozed-alarm details — is kept in the
app's private area without that additional encryption, protected by Android's app sandbox rather
than by a key. An optional biometric / screen-lock app lock is available in Settings.
Because no data is transmitted to or held by us, there is no server of ours that can be breached.
