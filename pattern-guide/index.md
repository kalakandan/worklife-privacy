# How to build your shift pattern

**Work & Life for Android** (also on iPhone as *WorkLife: Shift Work Calendar*) — takes about two minutes.

Your shift pattern is the one thing the app needs from you. Set it once, and Today, Schedule, your
alarms, your sleep window and your pay estimate all fill themselves in — nothing to enter by hand,
ever. Until it's set, Today shows no countdown and the calendar stays empty.

Prefer a full screen-by-screen walkthrough with every button labelled? [Download the PDF guide](../WorkAndLife-Pattern-Building-Guide.pdf).

## Where to find it

All four routes below open the same builder — pick whichever is closest to where you already are.

1. **The full route:** `Settings → Work rhythm → Manage all patterns → + New pattern`. Use this any
   time; you can keep as many saved patterns as you like and switch between them.
2. **Ready-made patterns:** `Settings → Work rhythm → Ready-made patterns` — pick a common rotation
   from a catalogue of 21, then edit it. Fastest if your rota is a standard one, like 3 Days / 3
   Nights / 6 Off.
3. **Your active pattern:** `Settings → Work rhythm → [your current pattern]` opens the same builder
   on the pattern you're already using, so you can change it.
4. **During first-time setup** on a brand-new install, the welcome flow offers "Build your pattern"
   and "Ready-made pattern" as cards — same builder, same catalogue.

Whichever way you get there, the builder itself is identical. Learn it once and every route is easy.

## Build your own, block by block

A **block** is a run of the same shift — "2 Days", "2 Nights", "4 Off". Most rotas are just two to
four blocks, added in order.

> **Worked example on this page — "4 on 4 off," an 8-day cycle**
> 2 × Day, 2 × Night, 4 × Off

1. Open the builder (see the four routes above). During first-time setup it opens with a sample
   week (5 Days, 2 Off) that you'll replace; from Settings → **New pattern** it opens empty.
2. Under **Add a shift**, tap the **Shift** row and roll the wheel to your first shift — **Day**. No
   shift that fits? Tap **+ Add your shift type** to create one: name it, set its **Starts** and
   **Ends** times, and pick a colour that reads clearly against the others.
3. Set **Starts** and **Ends** — we used 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. If those hours match a shift you
   already have, the builder says so and reuses it; different hours make a new one.
4. Set **How many days** this block runs — for our first block, 2 — then tap **+ Add 2 × Day**. It
   lands as its own row under "Your rhythm".
5. Repeat for the next block: tap **Shift**, roll to **Night**, set 6:00 PM to 6:00 AM, keep 2 days,
   tap **+ Add 2 × Night**.
6. Off days are added the same way — roll to **Off** (a day off has no times), set 4 days, tap
   **+ Add 4 × Off**.
7. First-time setup only: remove the sample blocks with their **×**. Your rhythm now reads
   "2 Day (12h) · 2 Night (12h) · 4 Off · 8-day cycle".
8. Give the pattern a name you'll recognise — we used `4 on 4 off`.
9. Set **day 1** — the one setting every future date is worked out from. In first-time setup, tap
   **Continue** and then tap the day you're on today. From Settings, use **Which day is day 1?**
   just under your blocks and set it to the real date your cycle starts.
10. Check the preview against a date you already know — setup's day-1 screen shows **the next 7
    days**; in Settings, the **2-month projection** sits further down the builder — so you catch a
    wrong "day 1" before saving, not after.
11. Finish: in first-time setup, tap **Continue** through the last two screens; from Settings, tap
    **Save & use**. Your calendar, Today's countdown and your automatic alarms all fill in
    immediately.

## The one setting to get right

Your pattern repeats in a cycle, and the app needs to know *where in that cycle today falls* — get
"which day is day 1" right and every future day lines up on its own.

> If your calendar ends up right in *shape* but wrong by a day or two, day 1 is almost always why.
> Fix it from `Settings → Work rhythm → [your pattern] → Edit` — the projection updates instantly so
> you can check before saving again.

## Things worth knowing

- **Blocks run in the order you add them.** Tap a block to change it where it is (then **Update
  this block**); to move one, remove it and add it again in the right place.
- **Save vs Save & use:** `Save` keeps the pattern without switching to it; `Save & use` keeps it
  *and* makes it active. Only one pattern drives your calendar at a time — the rest sit in "My
  patterns" waiting.
- **Overnight shifts are handled for you.** An end time earlier than the start time — 6 PM to 6 AM —
  is understood as a 12-hour overnight shift, not a mistake.
- **Nothing is lost by experimenting.** Every saved pattern keeps its own `Use` button, so switching
  back to an older one is always one tap away.

## Made a mistake, or your calendar looks wrong?

- **Today still says no pattern is set** — you haven't saved one yet; follow the steps above.
- **Days are in the wrong place** — the day-1 step was off. Open your pattern, correct it, check the
  projection, and save again.
- **You've saved more than one pattern** — only the one marked **ACTIVE** drives your calendar. Open
  the one you want and tap **Use**.
